# Pre-submission audit, 2026-08-19

Every numeric claim in `paper/main.tex` was recomputed from the sealed runs and
traced to a specific artifact. Method: micro and macro aggregation, matched and
unmatched cell sets, and per-budget sweeps were computed for every run that
carries a transcript, then each paper number was searched for across all of
them. A number that no aggregation reproduces is reported as unsupported rather
than assumed correct.

The measurements hold. The defects are in how the paper cites them: five
numbers in one subsection come from runs the paper does not use — one of which
it explicitly disowns two paragraphs later — and the subsection that would be
hardest for a reviewer to check rests on a run that does not ship.

## S1 — §5.2 quotes a superseded run and a different experiment

`\textbf{Mem0 consolidates}` and `\textbf{Graphiti retains but does not filter}`
report numbers that no current run produces:

| Paper | Traced to | Correct value (runs the paper uses) |
|---|---|---|
| Mem0 `transaction_as_of` $0.956$ | `external-mem0-grid-v1` (0.9561) | **0.952** |
| Mem0 `valid_time` $0.374$ | `external-mem0-grid-v1` (0.3744) | **0.364** |
| Graphiti exact $0.194$ | `external-graphiti-scale-v1` (0.1942) | **0.196** |
| Graphiti recall $0.944$ | `external-graphiti-scale-v1` (0.9439 macro) | **0.937** |
| "identical at $k{=}1$ ($0.385$)" | both above were 0.3846 | **0.379 vs 0.354 — not identical** |

`external-mem0-grid-v1` is the shared-history-database run that the *same
subsection* describes as corrupted under contention and re-run. The paragraph
announcing the correction quotes the uncorrected numbers.

The qualitative claim survives: at $k{=}1$ the Wilson intervals overlap heavily
(Mem0 $[0.314, 0.449]$, Graphiti $[0.290, 0.423]$), so "indistinguishable" is
supported. "Identical", and the single shared figure $0.385$, are not.

## S2 — §5.6 rests on a run that does not ship

Every number in External Validity comes from `longmemeval-e7-v2` (60 instances,
6 question types). Its seal verifies and its values match the paper exactly
(0.7238, 0.6221, 0.3136, 0.2867, 0.2208, 0.1512). But the run is **not in
`accepted-runs.json`**, is matched by the `experiments/runs/*` ignore rule with
no allowlist entry, and is therefore **untracked by git and absent from the
artifact package**. The two accepted LongMemEval runs hold 6 and 12 instances
across two question types and cannot produce the reported table.

For a track whose reviewers are a Reproducibility Committee, this is the most
serious finding: the section is unverifiable as shipped, and the run would be
lost to a clean checkout.

## S3 — Table 1's caption and body are different experiments

`tab:baselines` is captioned *"Internal baselines over 27 conditions (3 profiles
× 3 scales × 3 revision counts) × 10 seeds = 270 cells per system … deliberate
strawmen"* but its body is the seven **external** configurations over 484 cases.
Consequences:

- §5.1's prose cites this table for numbers that are not in it. Those numbers
  are themselves correct — `lifecycle-grid-robust-v1` gives append-only and
  full-context 0.915 recall / 0.272 exact, high-churn 0.255 / 0.662, oracle
  1/1/0 over exactly 27 conditions × 10 seeds — so the internal-baselines table
  is *missing*, not wrong.
- The external summary is duplicated between `tab:baselines` and `tab:reader`.

## S4 — Two numbers no artifact can produce

- **"rank~1 in 99.4% of cases"** — no transcript in any run carries a rank
  field. The claim cannot be recomputed offline at all.
- **Graphiti filtered "valid-time remains $0.093$"** — no run reproduces it
  under micro, macro, or any matched subset; `external-graphiti-filtered-v2`
  gives **0.114**.

Both contradict §7's claim that every reported figure is an aggregation over
sealed transcripts.

## S5 — Cognee latency quotes 38-sample smoke runs

$0.76$\,s / $7.93$\,s / $5.00$\,s come from `external-cognee-v1` and
`external-cognee-chunks-v1` (n = 37–38). The grid runs used for every other
Cognee number give **0.647 / 6.906 / 4.534** (n = 477/484). The conclusion —
Cognee inverts the write/read split — holds under either, but the paper switches
sample without saying so, and uses the smaller one.

## S6 — Stale system counts

- Setup, *Systems*: names only "Mem0 `0.1.118` and Graphiti `0.29.2`". LangMem,
  Cognee `1.4.2` and Hindsight are missing.
- Limitations: "Two external systems, one backbone" against the abstract's five.
- `tab:reader` is captioned "Five systems, and two of them read both ways" but
  omits both Hindsight rows, so it shows five rows with only one system read
  both ways.
- Limitations repeats its opening sentence nearly verbatim one paragraph later.

## S7 — Minor

- **Aggregation is mixed silently.** Tables declare case-weighted (micro) rates;
  the adjacent Graphiti-filter paragraph is macro (0.224 / 0.561 / 0.873 are all
  cell-averaged). Both are defensible; neither is labelled.
- **Figures are not byte-reproducible.** Regeneration produces identical text
  content but different bytes (PDF timestamps).
- **Coincidental collisions.** Mem0 0.603 / LangMem 0.576 round identically to
  internal last-write-wins 0.6028 / BM25 0.5763. Distinct measurements — they
  diverge at the 4th decimal — but adjacent tables invite the wrong reading.
- **Five ledger-cited runs are untracked**: `lifecycle-grid-v1`,
  `lifecycle-grid-v2`, `lifecycle-operations-v1`, `lifecycle-9406331b97a3`,
  `longmemeval-e7-v2`.
- `refs.bib` carries two uncited entries (`locomo`, `sql2011`).
